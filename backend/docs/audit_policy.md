# Audit Policy

## 1. Purpose

The Audit module provides attributable records of sensitive operations
performed within the Taunggyi City Church Community Management System.

The system shall retain audit events for sensitive and financial
create, edit, authorise, reject, and correct actions.

Audit records support:
- Accountability
- Financial history review
- Sensitive-operation investigation
- Reconstruction of who acted, what changed, and when

This document describes proposed Release 1 audit requirements.
Retention and archival policy require church leadership approval.

## 2. Audited Operations

The system shall audit the following operations.

### 2.1 Member Management
- Member creation
- Member editing
- Member transfer
- Sensitive member-data changes

### 2.2 Attendance
- Attendance session creation and lifecycle changes
- Attendance recording
- Attendance correction
- Visitor count creation and updates

### 2.3 Staff and Permissions
- Staff account creation and modification
- Staff role assignment and revocation
- Role and permission changes
- Sensitive account-management actions

### 2.4 Financial Operations
- Donation creation and editing
- Fund transaction creation and editing
- Financial authorisation
- Financial rejection
- Financial correction and reversal
- Financial adjustment authorisation

### 2.5 Other Sensitive Operations
Sensitive operations identified by the application security and
business requirements must also produce audit events.

## 3. Audit Event Fields

Each audit event should contain:

- Unique event identifier
- Actor identifier
- Action
- Target resource type
- Target resource identifier
- Event timestamp
- Appropriate before/after metadata
- Request correlation identifier, when available

The event must identify the operation and the affected resource.

Audit timestamps must be generated by the server.

## 4. Before/After Metadata

For audited edits, record appropriate before/after values.

Metadata must be limited to information necessary to understand
the change.

Do not include:
- Passwords
- Authentication tokens
- Secret keys
- Unnecessary donor identity
- Unnecessary sensitive member information
- Complete sensitive request payloads

Anonymous donation records must not store donor identity.

Protected values must not be exposed to users who lack permission
to view the corresponding financial or sensitive information.

## 5. Audit Event Integrity

Audit events must be immutable application records.

Ordinary application endpoints must not provide audit-event editing
or deletion.

Audit event creation must be coupled to the relevant business
transaction where required.

The following operations must commit atomically:

- Member creation/edit and its audit event
- Attendance marks and visitor count updates with their audit events
- Financial authorisation decision, current status update, and audit event

A failed transaction must not leave a committed sensitive change
without its required audit event.

Financial correction creation and authorisation use separate
transactions and preserve immutable history.

Database constraints and restricted database access should provide
additional protection against audit mutation.

## 6. Financial Audit History

Financial audit history must identify:

- Creator
- Editor
- Authoriser
- Actor
- Action
- Target financial record
- Timestamp
- Appropriate change metadata

Financial authorisation events must identify exactly one target.

Authorised financial source records remain unchanged.

Corrections and reversals must use linked adjustment records.
Their creation, decision, and report-impacting actions must be audited.

Financial audit history is accessible only to Pastor and Administrator.

Protected financial values must not be exposed to other roles.

## 7. Audit Access Control

### Administrator
May review general audit logs, subject to protected-field redaction.

### Pastor
May review financial audit history.

General audit access for Pastor requires an explicit permission
decision.

### Zone Leader
May not access general audit logs or financial audit history.

### Public Visitor
May not access audit logs or audit history.

Audit endpoints must enforce permissions on the backend.

Frontend visibility is not an access-control mechanism.

Unauthorized requests must be denied.

## 8. Audit Queries

Audit queries should support filtering by:
- Actor
- Action
- Resource type
- Resource identifier
- Date/time range

Audit history should be presented chronologically.

Financial history must preserve the relationship between:
- Original financial record
- Authorisation decision
- Correction or reversal
- Adjustment authorisation

Audit responses must expose only fields permitted for the requesting role.

## 9. Logging and Privacy

Audit records must contain enough information to reconstruct
sensitive actions without unnecessarily duplicating protected data.

Application logs must not contain:
- Passwords
- Authentication tokens
- Secret keys
- Complete sensitive financial records
- Unnecessary donor identity
- Unnecessary sensitive member information

Audit and operational logging are separate concerns.

Operational logs may record application errors and service health.
They must not be treated as a replacement for attributable audit events.

## 10. Retention and Archival

Audit retention and archival requirements are not yet approved.

Before production, church leadership and the project owner must
confirm:
- Retention duration for member audit records
- Retention duration for attendance audit records
- Retention duration for financial audit records
- Archival requirements
- Secure disposal requirements
- Backup retention and access controls

Until approved, no specific retention duration is established
by this document.

Audit records and backups must be protected against unauthorised
access or alteration.

## 11. Audit Review

Audit review must support investigation of sensitive and financial
operations.

Reviewers should be able to determine:
- Who performed the action
- What action was performed
- Which resource was affected
- When the action occurred
- What appropriate values changed
- Whether a financial decision or correction followed

Audit review must respect role permissions and protected-field
redaction.

## 12. Unresolved Decisions

The following require approval before production:

- Audit retention duration
- Member, attendance, and financial audit archival policy
- Secure disposal requirements
- General audit-log access for Pastor
- Exact audit metadata for each sensitive operation
- Audit export requirements
- Operational monitoring and audit-review responsibilities

Do not treat these decisions as approved policy until confirmed.
